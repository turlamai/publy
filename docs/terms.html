Publy service status and onboarding boundary

English · 简体中文

Public hosted purchasing opened on 2026-11-26: Personal, ¥29 per calendar month, one-time payment via Alipay, no automatic renewal. A purchase covers delivery of drafts to your own Official Account through the operated server for that paid month; it never includes mass-sending or public posting. Refunds go through Alipay's official channel, and a completed refund revokes the entitlement at the next verified check. The operated service is new: availability is provided on an effort basis, and problems are handled through the repository Issues. Do not submit account secrets anywhere in public.

CLI, default themes, Obsidian and self-hosting remain available under their existing source licences. A self-hosting operator must identify its own service, support and data handling; repository documentation does not automatically supply that operator’s customer terms.

Before a future sale, publish matching English/Chinese operator identity/contact, final tax/fee disclosure, metering, billing period and auto-renewal status, cancellation/refunds, failure/support scope and export/deletion procedures, then verify purchase through fulfilment. PRICING.md owns the implementation design, not a current sales contract.

See the current user path and privacy status.

Publy 服务状态与开通边界

English · 简体中文

公开托管购买已于 2026-11-26 开放：Personal 档，每个自然月 ¥29，支付宝一次付款，不自动续费。购买覆盖的是：当月通过代运营服务器向你自己的公众号投递草稿；绝不包含群发或公开发布。退款走支付宝官方渠道，退款完成后权益在下一次校验时撤销。代运营服务为新上线，可用性按尽力而为提供，问题通过仓库 Issues 反馈。请勿在任何公开场合提交账号秘密。

CLI、默认主题、Obsidian 与自托管继续按现行源码许可证使用。自托管运营者需明确自己的服务身份、支持和数据处理方式；本仓文档不自动构成其面向其他用户的服务条款。
