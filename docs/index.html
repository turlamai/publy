A simple workflow简单的使用流程

From a note to a draft,
with you in control.从笔记到草稿，
每一步都清楚。

Keep writing where you already do. Review the result locally, choose the account, and check the draft before publishing it yourself.不必迁移笔记。先在本地检查和预览，再选择公众号投递，最后到微信后台确认。

01 / WRITE

Write as usual照常写作

Keep your Markdown and images in their existing folders. Publy reads the file you select.Markdown 和图片留在原来的文件夹，Publy 只读取你选择的文章。

02 / REVIEW

Preview locally本地预览

Check attachments and review the formatted article before anything is sent.提前检查图片与封面，在投递之前预览排版效果。

03 / DELIVER

Send to drafts投递到草稿箱

Choose your account and create a draft. You decide when to review and publish in WeChat.选定公众号并创建草稿；何时检查、何时公开，由你决定。

Made for the details把细节照顾好

Less rearranging.
More writing.少一些搬运，
多一些写作。

A focused path from an existing document to a WeChat draft, with the important decisions left visible.围绕一篇已有文章，做好检查、排版和草稿投递，让重要的决定始终清晰可见。

Formatting排版

Built-in article themes, local images, code and math rendering.内置文章主题，处理本地图片、代码和公式。

Image posts图片消息

Turn structured Markdown into a deck of themed cards.将结构化 Markdown 渲染为主题卡片。

Clear outcomes结果清楚

A confirmed draft and an uncertain result are shown differently, so you can check before taking action.区分已确认和结果未知，遇到中断先核对，再决定后续操作。

Your files你的文件

Your notes stay in your own folders; Publy is not a replacement notebook.笔记仍由你在原处管理，Publy 不替你建立另一套笔记库。

Availability today当前可用情况

Local tools and self-hosting are free under their existing licences. The current setup is for developers; a polished standalone installer is still being prepared.本地工具与自托管按现有许可证免费使用。目前仍需开发者配置，面向普通用户的独立安装包正在准备中。

Public hosted registration and payment are not open yet. We will share details when the service is ready.公开托管注册与付款尚未开放，服务准备就绪后再公布详情。
