What it adds托管提供什么

Reliability, not lock-in.可靠投递，不锁定内容。

The paid service, when it exists, is for operational reliability and recovery — never for moving content between editors.付费服务（如果开放）卖的是运营可靠性与恢复能力，不是编辑器之间的搬运。

Fixed egress + credentials固定出口 + 凭据保管

A stable server IP accepted by WeChat, with the AppSecret kept server-side and never sent to the client.微信白名单认可的稳定 IP；AppSecret 只留在服务端，不下发客户端。

Draft recovery草稿恢复

Confirmed vs unknown outcomes, same-request retry and operator reconciliation against the WeChat draft box.区分已确认与结果未知；同请求重试；运营者按微信草稿箱核对。

Scheduling + quota排程与配额

Server-side scheduled drafts, cancellation before send and an atomic monthly allowance.服务端排程草稿、发送前取消，以及原子化的月度额度。

Still draft-only仍然只到草稿

No mass-send and no public posting. You review and publish in WeChat yourself.不群发、不公开发布；最终检查与发布仍由你在微信后台完成。

Why closed为什么暂不开放

Gates we won't skip.不会绕过的门禁。

  1. Merchant and legal admission for online payment in mainland China (business entity, ICP filing where required, payment-provider review).中国大陆线上收款所需的商户与合规准入（企业主体、必要的 ICP 备案、支付渠道审核）。
  2. Production entitlement checks through Commerce + Permit, refunds and quota enforcement — not a local “paid” flag.通过 Commerce + Permit 的生产级权益校验、退款与配额执行，而不是本地“已付款”标记。
  3. Proven reliability: fixed-egress pilot, backup/restore rehearsal, unknown-outcome recovery and service operations.可靠性验收：固定出口试点、备份恢复演练、未知结果恢复与服务运营。

These gates are tracked in the repository (issues #7, #8, #14, #17 and #44). Until they pass, the correct answer to “how do I buy?” is: you can't yet — use the free local/self-hosted path.这些门禁记录在仓库 issue #7、#8、#14、#17、#44。在全部通过之前，“怎么买”的正确答案是：暂时不能买，请使用免费的本地/自托管路径。
